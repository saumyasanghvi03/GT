# Pitch Questions - GT Round 2 (Team TECHONOMIST)

Below is a set of strategic, operational, financial, and cultural questions designed to pressure-test your turnaround blueprint for XYZ Mobiles. These are categorized by the dimensions of evaluation.

## 1. Strategic Direction & Positioning
**The Core Pivot: Hardware to Ecosystem**
*   **Q:** "You propose a pivot from a pure hardware player to an 'ecosystem' model (Slide 7) to drive margin expansion (12% → 35%). Ecosystem plays like Apple or Xiaomi rely on a massive, loyal active user base. Given XYZ’s *declining* market share and eroding trust, how do you build this install base fast enough to support a services business in just 3-5 years?"
*   **Q:** "You explicitly prioritize 'Domestic Recovery' over 'Aggressive International Expansion' (Slide 2), citing PLI benefits. However, scale is the primary driver of cost efficiency in mobile manufacturing. By limiting your footprint compared to global competitors who sell into multiple markets, do you risk a permanent cost disadvantage that PLI subsidies can't mask forever?"

**Gen-Z & Brand Relevance**
*   **Q:** "Gen-Z consumers (Slide 3) are notoriously brand-conscious and sticky (e.g., the 'blue bubble' effect). XYZ enters this fight with 'eroding consumer trust.' Beyond 'innovation' and 'ecosystem,' what is the specific *wedge* or hook that will compel a Gen-Z user to switch from a dominant incumbent to a recovering brand?"

## 2. Operational Excellence & Execution
**Supply Chain & PLI Reliance**
*   **Q:** "Your strategy heavily leverages the PLI scheme (Slide 2, Appx B) to mitigate execution risk. PLI is a government policy subject to annual review and geopolitical shifts. If the incentive structure changes or payouts are delayed (common in the industry), how does that impact your projected 4.2x capital efficiency (Slide 5)? Do you have a Plan B if the subsidy dries up?"
*   **Q:** "You mention 'Supply Chain Optimization' as a resilience flywheel (Slide 6). In a world of component shortages and trade wars, specifically how does XYZ Mobiles secure priority allocation of critical components (chipsets, displays) against larger players when your volumes are currently shrinking?"

## 3. Financial Sustainability
**Valuation & Projections**
*   **Q:** "Your financial model projects an IRR of 15-28% (Slide 5), beating B2B SaaS benchmarks. This is an incredibly aggressive target for a hardware-heavy turnaround. What are the top 3 sensitivity variables in your Monte Carlo simulation (Slide 9) that could swing this IRR below your cost of capital?"
*   **Q:** "You reference 'Services Transformation' similar to HPE GreenLake (Slide 7). GreenLake is a B2B consumption model. For a B2C mobile player, 'Services' usually means App Store commissions, subscriptions, or ads. Which specific specific 'Services' revenue streams are you banking on, and are your ARPU targets (Appx G) validated against *mid-tier* Android users, not just premium iPhone users?"

## 4. Culture & Talent
**Retention & Leadership**
*   **Q:** "You acknowledge rising attrition and disengaged employees. You mention 'India's semiconductor boom' (Slide 8) as a talent opportunity, but typically that boom *drains* talent from OEMs to chip firms. How specifically will you restructure compensation (beyond standard ESOPs) to retain your best engineers who are currently looking for the exit?"
*   **Q:** "Cultural resets are painful and slow. 'Aligning employees' (Slide 8) is easy to say but hard to do when profitability is cutting. Are you planning headcount reductions to achieve your cost discipline? If so, how do you reconcile layoffs with 'rebuilding culture'?"

## 5. The "Curveball" Questions (Narrative & Feasibility)
*   **Q:** "If you had only 50% of the requested capital, which pillar of this strategy would you cut first: The Domestic PLI expansion, the Ecosystem R&D, or the Talent restructuring? Why?"
*   **Q:** "You mention 'Quality Lapses' as a core historical issue. A reputation for bad quality takes years to wash off. Marketing can't fix a motherboard failure. What immediate, concrete operational changes are you making on the factory floor *tomorrow* to ensure the very next batch of phones doesn't have the same defects?"
*   **Q:** "Your 'Exit Strategy' (Appx L) mentions a valuation comparable to Zomato or Nykaa. These are platform/marketplace businesses. XYZ is fundamentally a manufacturing/product business. Is this valuation multiple realistic, or are you dressing up a hardware company as a tech platform to please investors?"

## 6. Presentation Specific Checks
*   **Slide 4 (Financial Model):** Ensure you can explain the inputs of your Monte Carlo simulation. Simply saying "82% success probability" isn't enough. Be ready to defend the standard deviation assumptions.
*   **Slide 9 (Risk):** You mention "three concurrent crises." Be specific about what these are (e.g., Currency devaluation + PLI delay + New competitor entrant).
