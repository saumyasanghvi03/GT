## SLIDE-2 - Strategic Alternatives: Why Domestic + PLI?
**The Logic:**
"We evaluated three paths: Aggressive International Expansion, Niche Luxury, and Domestic Recovery. We chose **Domestic Recovery boosted by PLI**. Why?
1.  **Lower Risk:** International expansion requires massive marketing spend we don't have.
2.  **Higher Efficiency:** PLI exports hit $1.8B/month with 40% lower execution risk.
3.  **Capital Protection:** Subsidies protect our bottom line while we fix our internal operations."

**Boardroom Defense:**
"If you ask why we aren't going global yet, the answer is simple: You don't invade foreign markets when your home castle is burning."

## SLIDE-3 - Customer Cohort: Winning Gen-Z
**The Insight:**
"Gen-Z isn't buying phones; they are buying entry tickets to a digital life. Their ecosystem spending will hit **$250B by 2025**. Our 'Erosion of Trust' is actually an opportunity to re-brand as the 'Homegrown Hero' that understands their needs better than a generic global brand."

**Key Data to Hit:**
*   **$250B:** Gen-Z Ecosystem Spend.
*   **Strategy:** "We stop selling specs (Camera MP) and start selling value (Gaming, Content, Identity)."
